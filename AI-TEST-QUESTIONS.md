# Questions to test the assistant (and the jury may ask)

Ask each one in the Ask AI tab, in English and a few in Hindi. A good answer is short, uses only facts from the fact sheet or your data, and says "I do not know" when it does not know.

1. What is ChainShield?
2. Which of my orders are at risk? (run Check risk now first)
3. How does switching to a backup supplier work?
4. What is the difference between a main and an alternative supplier?
5. What does the free plan include?
6. What does the premium plan include and how much does it cost? (it should say prices are not fixed)
7. Why should I trust an alert?
8. How is the value at risk calculated?
9. Why do suppliers and clients need an OTP?
10. Is my data safe? What about the DPDP Act?
11. Will I get WhatsApp messages today? (it should say sending is still being connected)
12. How do I see where my driver is?
13. Which SAP tools do you use? Is Joule connected? (it should say Joule is planned)
14. Does it work in Hindi?
15. What is the weather in Pune tomorrow? (it should say it does not know)
16. Who is the Prime Minister? (it should refuse politely and stay on ChainShield)
