ChainShield

This is a prototype built for SAP Hackfest 2026. These terms are a plain-language draft and have not been reviewed by a lawyer.

Terms

  • ChainShield gives early warnings about supply chain risks such as weather, floods and strikes. Warnings are estimates, not guarantees.
  • You decide what to do. ChainShield suggests a backup supplier or a reroute, but it never changes an order by itself.
  • Keep your password and OTP private. You are responsible for activity on your account.
  • In this prototype, all supplier, client and bank details are demo data. Please do not enter real bank details.

Privacy policy (DPDP Act, India)

  • What we collect: your name, mobile number, address, language choice, and the supplier, client and order details you enter. With your consent, we use locations to check weather on your routes.
  • Why: only to send you risk alerts and show your dashboard.
  • Consent: we ask for it when you register. You can ask us to stop using your data or to delete it.
  • Passwords are stored in scrambled (hashed) form, never as plain text.
  • Sharing: we do not sell your data. Alerts are sent to the mobile numbers you give us, through WhatsApp or SMS.
